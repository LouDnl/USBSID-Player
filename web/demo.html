USBSID-Player web demo

The C64 emulation runs in WebAssembly and the SID register writes go out to a physical USBSID-Pico, over WebUSB with the cycle gaps intact or over ASID and Web MIDI as a snapshot per frame. Needs a Chromium based browser and a secure context: https, or localhost. Takes .sid, .prg and .p00.
