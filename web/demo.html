USBSID-Player Web Demo

Loads the WASM player, steps the C64 emulation once per (wall-clock) frame, and streams the SID register writes to a USBSID-Pico over WebUSB or ASID (Web MIDI). Requires a Chromium-based browser and a secure context (https or localhost). Supports .sid and .prg/.p00 files.
